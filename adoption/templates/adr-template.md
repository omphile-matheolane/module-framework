## Context

What is the situation that requires a decision? Describe the
forces at play, including the constraints, the drivers, and any
prior decisions that shape the current context. Keep this
concrete: specific problems, specific signals from observability
or governance, specific conversations that prompted the decision.

This section should be enough for a reader a year from now to
understand why the decision was being made, without needing to
reconstruct the context from elsewhere.

## Decision

If the decision follows from the evolution decision model, include

assessment is sufficient if the assessment has its own document.
